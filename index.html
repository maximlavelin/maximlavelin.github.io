NYC Taxi Fleet Optimizer

Linear Regression · XGBoost · Lag features · Chronological validation
  • Developed a forecasting pipeline to predict hourly NYC taxi demand, framed as a fleet-planning problem: how many vehicles should be available at a given time?
  • Compared a transparent Linear Regression baseline against XGBoost, using lag, rolling and calendar features.
  • Used a chronological Jan–Sep / Oct / Nov–Dec 2024 split rather than random sampling to reproduce realistic forecasting conditions.
  • XGBoost achieved 546 MAE versus 728 for Linear Regression, reducing prediction error by approximately 25%.

Vehicle Safety Intelligence

TF-IDF · CNN · BiLSTM · Isotonic calibration · Bootstrap CIs
  • Developed a text-classification pipeline for detecting complaints describing crashes, fires, injuries or fatalities from NHTSA consumer complaint narratives.
  • Compared three modeling approaches — TF-IDF + MLP, CNN and BiLSTM — on roughly 300K complaints with a highly imbalanced 6.61% serious-failure rate.
  • The CNN produced the best ROC-AUC (0.9513), while bootstrap analysis showed that its improvement over the strong MLP baseline was not statistically significant.
  • Split observations by complaint ID (ODINO) to prevent leakage, calibrated probabilities using isotonic regression, and tested performance across vehicle-component subgroups.

Unsupervised learning · Customer analytics

Retail Consultant

PCA · K-Means vs. HAC · Apriori · STL · LOF · SVD
  • Built an end-to-end customer analytics pipeline from 805K Online Retail II transactions, covering segmentation, basket analysis, anomaly detection and recommendations.
  • Compared K-Means with hierarchical clustering; HAC achieved a 0.604 silhouette score versus 0.321, revealing 5,871 regular buyers and seven high-value wholesale customers.
  • Those seven customers generated approximately 14× the revenue per customer and were all identified by LOF as anomalous purchasing behavior.
  • Apriori produced cross-sell rules reaching 28 lift, while SVD collaborative filtering improved recommendation RMSE by 1.7%.

Public Transport Accessibility

GeoPandas · GTFS · CBS Census 2022 · Getis-Ord Gi* · ArcGIS Pro
  • Built a geospatial accessibility model combining CBS Census demographics with GTFS public-transport schedules across 2,179 Israeli statistical areas.
  • Constructed separate transport-need and transport-supply indices, allowing areas with vulnerable populations and inadequate service to be identified.
  • The analysis showed that stop coverage is already around 94% nationally, shifting the accessibility problem from distance to transit toward frequency of service.
  • Identified 168 critical high-need/low-access areas and used Getis-Ord Gi* spatial statistics to locate statistically significant clusters.

Web Tracking & AI Profiling

Feature engineering · K-Means · DBSCAN · Chi-square testing
  • Analyzed tracker behavior across 10,000 websites using unsupervised learning and statistical testing.
  • Google trackers appeared on 99.2% of sites, while News/Portal sites averaged 10.85 trackers, compared with only 3.06 for Government sites.
  • K-Means separated websites into two tracking-intensity clusters with a 0.467 silhouette score, while DBSCAN identified unusually tracker-heavy sites.
  • Used these results to examine how algorithmic segmentation can transform continuous behavioral differences into discrete profiles.
